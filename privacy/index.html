Driftrealm Studio

Privacy Policy

Effective 11 October 2026

Driftrealm Studio makes calm instrumental music videos over painted sci-fi worlds. To publish them, it runs a small private tool on its own computer that uploads its own videos to its own accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. This policy explains what that tool handles.

Whose data

The tool is used only by Driftrealm Studio, for its own accounts. It has no users, no sign-up and no public interface. It does not collect, store or share any information about other people, including the people who watch or interact with our videos.

What it handles

  • The sign-in permission (access token) each platform grants to Driftrealm Studio's own account, kept only on our own computer and used only to upload our videos and read their upload status.
  • Basic details of our own account that the platform returns at sign-in (such as its name and ID).
  • Our own videos, titles and descriptions.

What it does not do

It does not read, collect or analyse anyone else's profile, messages, followers or activity, does not use cookies or tracking, and does not sell or share data with anyone. Viewing our videos on a platform is governed by that platform's own privacy policy.

Removing access

We can revoke the tool's access at any time in each platform's account settings; doing so stops all uploads.
